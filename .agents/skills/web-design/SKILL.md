# Web Design Skill & Master Playbook

This skill establishes industry-leading web design principles and workflows for building modern, high-converting, visually stunning websites that avoid generic "AI slop" and deliver production-grade craftsmanship.

---

## 1. Core Visual Principles & Anti-Slop Guidelines

### A. Grounded Realism Over Sci-Fi Tropes
- **No Fantasy Metaphors**: Never use sci-fi energy orbs, glowing reactor cores, or cartoonish lightning bolts for real-world service businesses.
- **Authentic Subject Matter**: Focus on the real craftsmanship of the trade: precision circuit panels, real tools, certified safety gear, clean conduit runs, authentic branded vehicles, and actual team operations.
- **Commercial Photography Standards**: When using imagery, prioritize clean 8K commercial photography with natural lighting, accurate depth of field, and grounded color grading.

### B. The 60-30-10 Color Rule
- **60% Dominant Base**: Deep charcoal, obsidian black (#090d12), or clean crisp whites/slates for backgrounds.
- **30% Secondary Surfaces**: Subtle frosted cards (rgba(15, 23, 36, 0.75)), borders (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)), and muted text.
- **10% High-Conversion Accent**: High-visibility signature color (e.g., electrical amber #f59e0b or emergency red #ef4444) reserved exclusively for primary CTAs, active status pills, and focal highlights.

## 2. Hero Section Architecture

### The High-Impact Split Hero Formula
1. **Left 50% (The Conversion Engine)**:
   - **Service Territory Pill**: [📍 PROUDLY SERVING GREATER MONTREAL & SOUTH SHORE]
   - **Oversized Headline**: Punchy, clear, high-contrast headline with signature accent word (e.g., ELECTRICAL EXPERTS YOU CAN COUNT ON).
   - **Value Proposition Subtitle**: 2 concise sentences highlighting licensing, experience, upfront pricing, and guarantees.
   - **Action Row**: Primary high-contrast CTA button + secondary friction-free phone/quote button.
   - **Trust Ribbon**: Real licensing numbers (CMEQ, RBQ), insured guarantees, and 24/7 emergency dispatch status.
2. **Right 50% (The Visual Anchor)**:
   - High-resolution, real-world focal photography (e.g., master electrician precision-wiring a circuit breaker box wearing a company-branded hardhat).
   - Seamless gradient fade on the left edge into the dark background, ensuring 100% text legibility.

## 3. Bento Box & Information Architecture

- **Modular Asymmetrical Grids**: Group offerings into logical, bite-sized cards that guide the user's eye naturally.
- **Visual Weight**: Give the highest-ticket or most frequent service double width (span-2).
- **Tactile Depth**:
  - Border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08) in dark mode.
  - Backdrop filter: backdrop-filter: blur(16px).
  - Subtle hover lift: transform: translateY(-4px) with gentle amber shadow.
  - Physical elevation of inner badges: translateZ(20px).

---

## 4. Typography Hierarchy

- **Display Headings**: Bold, confident sans-serif with tight tracking (letter-spacing: -0.03em) and high contrast.
- **Body Copy**: Highly legible font at 1rem (16px) with generous line-height (1.6em to 1.75em).
- **Data & Credentials**: Monospace or clean uppercase micro-labels (font-mono, text-xs, tracking-wider) for license numbers (RBQ, CMEQ) and technical specifications.

---

## 5. Conversion UX & Local Service Business Optimization

1. **Persistent Emergency Access**: For trades (electricians, plumbers, HVAC), always keep a persistent 1-tap call button to the 24/7 dispatch line visible on both desktop and mobile headers.
2. **Visual Quote Intake**: Allow users to photograph their current equipment (e.g., electrical panel) for remote assessment before dispatch.
3. **Geographic Reassurance**: Explicitly list key service cities (e.g., Montreal, Laval, Longueuil, Brossard, Boucherville) so visitors immediately know they are in the service area.

---

## 6. Accessibility & Performance Checklist (WCAG AA)

- [x] Text contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against backgrounds.
- [x] All images have descriptive alt text.
- [x] Focus states clearly visible for keyboard navigation (:focus-visible).
- [x] Respect prefers-reduced-motion: reduce by disabling non-essential parallax or video auto-play.
- [x] Zero layout shifts (CLS < 0.1) with fixed image dimensions.

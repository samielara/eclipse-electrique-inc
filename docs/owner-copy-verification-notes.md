## 2. Business Owner Questions to Unlock Upgraded Claims

Before promoting any bracketed claim to production, obtain explicit confirmation from the owner on these three items:

### Question 1: Pricing Model & Quotes
* **Unconfirmed Claim**: `[with upfront, transparent pricing] / [avec tarification transparente]`
* **Owner Question**: *"Do you offer fixed/upfront quotes before starting work, or do you bill standard time and materials / hourly rates? Do you want to advertise 'upfront pricing' or 'transparent estimates'?"*
* **Regulatory Context**: Under the Quebec *Consumer Protection Act* (Loi sur la protection du consommateur), advertising "no surprises" or "upfront pricing" creates a binding expectation. If extra fees or hourly variances apply, this claim must be adjusted or qualified.

### Question 2: Emergency Response Commitment
* **Unconfirmed Claim**: `[with rapid dispatch] / [avec intervention rapide]`
* **Owner Question**: *"Do you commit to a specific dispatch window (for example, within 60 to 90 minutes) for 24/7 emergencies, or should the copy strictly state that the 24/7 emergency telephone line is answered around the clock without promising physical dispatch speed?"*
* **Regulatory Context**: Stating rapid dispatch or response time guarantees without confirmed on-call staffing capacity creates liability during peak storm/outage events.

### Question 3: Service Territory Phrasing
* **Unconfirmed Claim**: `[and 50 service areas] / [et 50 secteurs desservis]`
* **Owner Question**: *"The website router supports 50 distinct municipal sector pages across Montreal, North Shore, and South Shore. Do you want to actively highlight '50 service areas' as a marketed metric in the hero, or do you prefer the broader regional phrasing 'Greater Montreal, North Shore, and South Shore'?"*
* **Context**: Broad regional phrasing avoids customer disputes if an edge municipality requires special travel fees.

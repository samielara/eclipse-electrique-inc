# Competitor-informed content roadmap

Reviewed 2026-09-08 for the Eclipse électrique website refresh. This is an editorial and information-architecture reference, not a source for copied text, imagery, client names, testimonials, prices, certifications or manufacturer claims.

## Patterns worth adopting

The strongest electrical references make service depth easy to scan, then support it with project evidence, helpful articles, service-area pages and a clear request path:

- [Lizotte Électrique](https://lelectricien.ca/) exposes granular residential, commercial and industrial services alongside projects, a blog, coverage areas and a quote CTA.
- [Ozen Électrique](https://ozenelectrique.com/) combines residential/commercial/industrial categories with a service-area structure, testimonials and a detailed project-intake form.
- [Acadia Électrique](https://www.acadiaelectrique.ca/) leads with a compact “why choose us” proof block, service specifics, testimonials, hours and contact details.

Fire and security references use the same architecture at a systems-and-sectors level:

- [Protect Fire](https://www.protectfire.ca/) organizes solutions by service and industry, adds a site-assessment CTA, testimonials and a simple three-step process.
- [Sécurité Novateck](https://securitenovateck.com/) makes systems, sectors, projects, emergency planning and service territory discoverable from the main navigation.
- [Spartan Fire](https://www.spartanfire.ca/) gives each system a concrete equipment scope and explains technician training and inspection standards.

Thermography references are especially strong on education and evidence:

- [Le Thermographe](https://lethermographe.com/) explains what an inspection detects, who benefits, report timing and the difference between independent inspection and repair work.
- [Infrarouge Kelvin](https://infrarougekelvin.com/) uses case examples and visual findings to connect thermography to avoided downtime and maintenance decisions.

## Changes applied in this release

- Added a bilingual homepage capability summary. It groups Eclipse’s already-published work into installation/upgrades, networks/protection and continuity/diagnosis so visitors can recognize their need before opening a service page.
- Kept the existing service and city detail routes as the SEO foundation. The header continues to link each service and each published city to its own page.
- Kept proof limited to facts already documented for Eclipse: RBQ 5582-0096-01, licence issued in 2008, Greater Montréal coverage and the published emergency line.

## Next content releases (owner approval required)

1. Add an approved project library with location, property type, scope, constraints, photos and outcome for representative residential, commercial and industrial work.
2. Add service-specific guides and FAQs for high-intent questions such as panel upgrades, generator planning, emergency troubleshooting and insurance inspections.
3. Add a short “how it works” request checklist and a richer intake form only after the company confirms its lead-handling and privacy process.
4. Add thermography, EV charging, fire/security or manufacturer-specific pages only after Eclipse confirms that each is offered and supplies the credentials, equipment scope and approved copy.
5. Add a bilingual insights/blog area once the company can maintain useful, locally relevant articles; empty or auto-generated pages should not be published.

## Guardrails

Do not publish competitor wording, images, prices, client logos, review excerpts, response-time guarantees, warranty promises, Generac authorization, thermography certifications or fire/security affiliations until Eclipse supplies permission or first-party evidence.
